Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Drag the pieces from the tray onto the 8×8 board. Fill a full row or column to clear it. Clearing a line made of one color triggers a Prism blast that removes every gem of that color. In levels, complete the goals before you run out of moves.

How does the leaderboard work?

Only Endless mode counts. Your single best score of the week, the month and all time is shown with your nickname and flag. Scores count even if you leave a game early. The top 100 players of each week and month receive booster prizes the next time they open the game.

How do I change my nickname or flag?

Open Settings › Edit profile.

How do I report or block a player?

Open the leaderboard and tap the player's name. Report sends the name to us for review and hides the player for you; Block only hides the player. You can unblock everyone in Settings.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open Settings › Delete account. Your nickname, scores and prizes are removed from our server immediately and permanently. Uninstalling the game removes all data stored on your device.

I changed phones. Can I keep my progress?

Level progress is stored on your device and is not synced. Your leaderboard entries stay on the leaderboard until the period ends.

Does the game need the internet?

No. All levels and Endless mode work offline. The leaderboard and prizes update when you are back online.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

Blockvia is free. You can watch a short video ad to continue a game, get an extra booster or double your daily gift, and a short ad may appear between games. A one-time Remove Ads purchase turns off ads between games and makes continues free. Booster packs are optional purchases. Bought on a new device? Use Settings › Restore purchases.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If you are in a region where consent is required, open Settings › Ad privacy options. You can also turn off tracking for Blockvia in your iPhone's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
