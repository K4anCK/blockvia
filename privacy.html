Privacy Policy

Applies to the Blockvia app for iPhone · Last updated: October 29, 2026

Summary

Blockvia is a puzzle game. Your level progress and settings stay on your device. To show the global leaderboard, we store an anonymous account ID, the nickname and flag you choose, and your Endless mode scores. There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics SDKs, and we never sell or share your data.

Data we collect

  • Anonymous account ID. Created automatically the first time you join the leaderboard so your scores belong to you. It is not linked to your name, email address, phone number or Apple ID.
  • Nickname and country flag. Chosen by you and shown to other players on the leaderboard.
  • Endless mode scores. Your best score of the week, the month and all time, plus the time it was set.
  • Reports. If you report another player's nickname, we keep the report (the reported name and your anonymous ID) for moderation.

We use this data only to run the leaderboard, award weekly and monthly booster prizes, and prevent cheating and offensive nicknames. It is not used for advertising or tracking and is not combined with data from other apps or websites.

Data we don't collect

We don't collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, advertising identifier or device identifiers. The game does not use cookies or third-party analytics.

Data stored on your device

Level progress, stars, settings, boosters and the list of players you blocked are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Service provider and security

Leaderboard data is stored with our backend provider Supabase, Inc., which processes it on our behalf. All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by access rules so that players can change only their own entries.

Retention and deletion

We keep your leaderboard data until you delete your account. You can do this at any time in Settings › Delete account: your account, nickname, scores and prizes are deleted from our server immediately and permanently. Reports are deleted automatically when the reporting or the reported account is deleted. A nickname reported by three different players is replaced with a neutral name. You can also request deletion or a copy of your data by emailing us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data and to object to its processing. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Blockvia is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the anonymous data described above. Nicknames are filtered for inappropriate words, and players can report and block others.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
